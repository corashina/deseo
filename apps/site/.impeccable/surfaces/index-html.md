## Scope and mode

Scope: the public information site in `apps/site`: `/`, `/invite`, `/support`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, and the 404 page.
Mode: Persuade. A visitor learns what Deseo is and how an invitation works. Bolder moves are welcome here.

## Audience and job

A person who got a partner link, or a person who heard of Deseo. They decide to open the invitation or come back at launch.

## Constraints

- Static HTML with no page JavaScript.
- Same palette and icon as the portal. Styles live in `apps/site/src/site.css`.
- No launch domain yet. Do not add canonical URLs, store links, or claims the project cannot prove.
- Keep the Lighthouse 100 scores.
