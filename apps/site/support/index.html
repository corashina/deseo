Pairing

How do I invite my partner?

Create an invitation in the app. Share the link or the 12-character code. The invitation expires after 24 hours.

The invitation does not work

The invitation can be expired or already used. Ask your partner to create a new one.

Can I join with my own invitation?

No. Your partner must use a second account.

Can I join a second pair?

No. Each account can join one active pair. End the current pair in Settings first.

Permissions

Notifications

Deseo works without notifications. Without them, you get no reminders or message alerts on that phone.

To change this, open your phone’s settings, then select Deseo.

Photos

Deseo needs photo access to add a memory. If you denied it, allow it in your phone’s settings.

Failed uploads

A photo did not upload

Keep Deseo open while photos upload. If an upload stops, select Retry or remove the failed photo.

Why is my memory not shown?

Deseo publishes a memory only after every photo uploads. Your partner sees nothing before that.
