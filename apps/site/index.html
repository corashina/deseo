Plan the week

Keep shared events, private events, important dates, and reminders in one calendar.

Each person has a fixed color and a visible name. Shared task lists show who does what and when.

Talk in one place

You and your partner have one conversation. Each person can pin one message as a note.

A message shows Sent only after Deseo saves it.

Keep your moments

Add one to five photos with a date and an optional caption.

The author can change the caption or delete the memory. Your partner can view it and save a copy.

Your privacy

  • A pair has two people. Each account can join one active pair.
  • Private events stay with their author. Your partner sees shared content only.
  • Either person can end the pair without the other person’s approval.
  • Deseo has no ads, public feeds, streaks, or relationship scores.

Questions

When can I download Deseo?

Deseo is not in the App Store or Google Play yet. Store links will appear on this page after release.

Who can see what I add?

Your partner sees shared events, tasks, messages, and memories. Private events stay private.

Can more than two people join?

No. Each pair has two people. Each account can join one active pair.

How do I join my partner?

Your partner creates an invitation in the app. Open the link or enter the 12-character code. An invitation expires after 24 hours.
