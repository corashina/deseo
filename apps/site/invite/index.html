Install Deseo

Deseo is not in the App Store or Google Play yet. When it is available:

  1. Install Deseo from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Open the original invitation link again.
  3. Sign in with the code that Deseo sends to your email.
  4. Check your partner’s name, then select Join pair.

The app store does not pass the invitation to Deseo. Open the original link again after you install.

What you share

  • Your partner sees shared events, tasks, messages, and memories.
  • Private events stay with their author.
  • Either person can end the pair without the other person’s approval.

See what Deseo does

Already have Deseo?

Open the invitation link on the phone that has Deseo. You can also enter the 12‑character code in the app.

Invitation does not work?

An invitation expires after 24 hours. Each invitation works one time. Ask your partner to create a new one.
